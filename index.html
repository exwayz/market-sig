Market Signal reads the War Era economy. Signal ≠ financial advice — trade responsibly.

How Market Signal works

Market Signal watches every tradable commodity in the War Era economy and turns it into a 7-level signal, from Strong Sell to Strong Buy.

Data

Every few minutes the collector snapshots the index price and the order book for all 23 commodities, plus a live trade feed. Each item also keeps 30 days of daily aggregates (average price, volume, transactions).

What moves the signal

  • Trend — price position vs 5/10/20-day averages and short-term momentum.
  • RSI — a blend of momentum and mean-reversion (oversold buys, overbought sells).
  • Pressure — bid vs ask depth imbalance in the order book.
  • Volume — does recent volume confirm or contradict the trend.
  • Intraday — movement since the last snapshot.

Confidence

A 0–100% confidence reflects liquidity (spread + depth), how aligned the components are, and the quality of the history.
